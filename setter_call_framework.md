# Setter Call Framework

**Mode:** Setter
**Purpose:** Reference script for the Setter game mode. Guides structure of a discovery call whose goal is to (1) understand the prospect's problem/pain, and (2) position a second call with a closer as the solution.

> Note: This is not meant to be used word-for-word. The questions are examples of the KIND of question to ask at each stage. Following the STRUCTURE (stage order) matters more than the exact wording. Don't let the questions get in the way of a real conversation.

---

## Stage Order

1. Warm Open & Framing
2. Hook / Reason-for-Reaching-Out
3. Situation Discovery
4. Problem Discovery
5. Solution Awareness
6. Goals & Vision
7. Cost of Inaction
8. Transition
9. Pitch

---

## Stage 1: Warm Open & Framing

**Goal:** Open the call in a neutral, low-pressure tone and set expectations for how the call will go.

**Example lines:**
- "Hey, is this {{CLIENT}}?"
- "Hey {{CLIENT}}, it's {{NAME}} from {{COMPANY_NAME}}. Saw you responded to one of our videos about possibly becoming a remote setter, and I had a few minutes so I thought I'd touch base and see if I might be able to help. Got a few minutes?"
- "Cool, so what I find works best on these calls is just to get a better understanding of what's going on with you and where you're trying to go — then, based on that, I can either get you more info, connect you with the right people, whatever would be most helpful for you. Cool?"

---

## Stage 2: Hook / Reason-for-Reaching-Out

**Goal:** Understand what triggered the prospect to respond in the first place.

**Example questions:**
- "When you saw that video, what caught your attention and motivated you to reach out in the first place?"

---

## Stage 3: Situation Discovery

**Goal:** Understand their current situation before digging into problems.

**Example questions:**
- "What do you do now?"
- "How long?"
- "Do you like it?"
  - If yes: "What do you like about it?"
  - Two Truths technique: "It seems like things are going fairly well — why even consider changing?"

---

## Stage 4: Problem Discovery

**Goal:** Surface what's not working, and how it's personally impacting them.

**Example questions:**
- "What don't you like about it?"
- Clarify: "What do you mean? / Tell me more. / In what way? / Can you elaborate?"
- "You said '{{THEIR_WORDS}}' — what did you mean by that?"
- "Anything else you don't like?" (if needed)
- Clarify again (if asked previously)
- Chunk down on the problem (optional, go more specific)
- Personal question: "What impact has that had on you? In what way?"
- "How long have you felt this way? How long has this been going on?"
- "How long have you been looking to change?"
- "Can I ask you a personal question? Has that had an impact on you? What's happening?"

---

## Stage 5: Solution Awareness

**Goal:** Find out what they've already tried, and why change is a priority now.

**Example questions:**
- "What have you tried or looked into?"
- "Why didn't that work out? / How did it go? What held you back?"
- "Why is it so important to fix this now? After all this time, what's got you keeping on keeping on?"
- "You've been doing this for {{X}} years — sounds like you've really wanted to change. What's happening now that's making this a priority?"

---

## Stage 6: Goals & Vision

**Goal:** Understand what they're working toward and why it matters to them.

**Example questions:**
- "Let's say you find a way to fix the problem — what would you need to make to replace your current income?"
  - "So you make {{X}} now?"
- "What's next for you? What's the bigger vision here?"
- "Why is that goal important? What do you want to do with that kind of money? What kind of impact will that have on your life?"
- "Anything else?"

---

## Stage 7: Cost of Inaction

**Goal:** Create urgency by connecting their timeline to the cost of staying where they are.

**Example questions:**
- "How long has that been a goal?"
- "How long until you get there on your current path?"
- Challenge them if they give a weak answer.
- "Is that why you're possibly looking to make a change?"
- "Why now, though?" (if necessary)

---

## Stage 8: Transition

**Goal:** Shift from discovery into presenting remote setting as the solution, framed around what they've shared.

**Example script:**
- "Do you mind if I share some thoughts?"
- (Paint current state vs. desired state)
- "Honestly, based on what you're telling me, I think being a remote setter makes a ton of sense for you — you know how you were saying {{PAIN/PROBLEM}} and how that's making you feel {{FEELING}}."
- "It sounds like more than anything you just want {{GOAL}}, and I think you'd do really well with remote setting because {{FIT_REASON}}."
- [Sell them on the opportunity — remote setting]
- Pause — "Is this something you can see yourself doing?"

---

## Stage 9: Pitch (Book the Closer Call)

**Goal:** Book the next call with a closer.

**Example script:**
- "So what I think makes the most sense is for you to talk to one of our coaches. Their job is two things:"
  - "Walk through the specifics of remote setting and map it to your goals — really explain what a path into this industry looks like."
  - "Get a deeper understanding of what you really want and whether this makes sense for your life."
  - "If it's a fit and this is something you can really see yourself doing, that's when they'll go over investment and timeline."
- "How does that sound?"
- "Great — I've got {{TIME_OPTION_1}} or {{TIME_OPTION_2}}."
