Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

This policy explains what Sales Camp AI does with your personal data. The controller is Simon Myhr, a sole trader based in Sweden, reachable at simmyh02@gmail.com.

The short version: we store your Google account basics, your training transcripts and your progress. Your training conversations are sent to Anthropic's AI to play the prospect and grade the call. We do not sell your data and we do not use it to train AI models. You can download everything we hold, or delete your account outright, from Settings → Your data.

1. What we collect

  • Account. Your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier, received from Google when you sign in.
  • Training data. Call transcripts, scores, feedback, saved lessons, unlocked skills, the offer and persona you picked, and your chosen conversation language.
  • Billing. Your plan tier and your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers. We never see or store your card details — Stripe handles the entire payment.
  • Technical. IP address and request metadata, used for rate limiting, security and error logs.
  • Local storage. Your sign-in token, theme and language preference are stored in your browser. These are functional only. We run no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

2. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To run the Service — creating your account, generating and grading training calls, tracking progress, enforcing session limits. Basis: performance of our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To take payment — subscriptions, renewals, failed-payment handling. Basis: contract, and legal obligation for accounting records.
  • To keep the Service up and safe — rate limiting, abuse prevention, debugging. Basis: our legitimate interest in a working, non-abused product (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To contact you about your account, a failed payment or a material change. Basis: contract and legitimate interest.

3. Who else processes your data

We use the processors below. Each is bound by a data processing agreement, and transfers outside the EEA rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

Worth saying plainly: the words you type or speak in a training call leave our systems and are processed by a third-party AI provider. Do not put real customer names, real contact details, confidential business information or any special-category personal data into a training call.

Anthropic does not use data submitted through its API to train its models. We do not sell your data, and we share it with no one beyond the processors listed above except where the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

Account and training data are kept while your account exists. Deleting your account from Settings → Your data erases your profile, scores, lessons, saved calls and call history immediately, and cancels any active subscription. The exception is records we must keep for accounting and tax purposes: paid invoices stay with Stripe for seven years under Swedish law. You can also email us and we will do the same within 30 days. Server logs are kept for a short operational period and then rotated out.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, export it in a portable format, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Export and deletion are self-serve under Settings → Your data — the export is a JSON file containing every record tied to your account. For anything else, email simmyh02@gmail.com and we will respond within one month. If you think we have handled your data badly you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or the authority where you live.

6. Security

Traffic runs over HTTPS. Sessions use signed tokens. Database access is restricted and credentials live in environment variables, never in the code. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your rights we will notify you and the regulator as the GDPR requires.

7. Children

The Service is not for under-16s and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will tell you by email or in the app before the change takes effect.
