What to write about

  • Support — something is broken, a call would not start, a score looks wrong. Include your account email and roughly when it happened.
  • Billing — refunds, double charges, an invoice you need. Most billing changes are faster to do yourself in Settings → Plan → Manage billing.
  • Privacy and GDPR — a copy of your data, a correction, or full deletion. Say which you want and we will confirm when it is done.
  • Everything else — feedback, a feature you need, a bug that annoys you. It gets read.

Response time

We aim to reply within 3 business days. Privacy requests are answered within one month, as the GDPR requires.
