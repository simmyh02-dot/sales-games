Refund Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

Short version: subscriptions are monthly, you can cancel yourself in two clicks, and if something goes wrong, email us.

Cancelling

Go to Settings → Plan → Manage billing. That opens Stripe's portal, where you can cancel, switch plan, update your card or download invoices. Cancelling stops the next charge; you keep your paid access until the end of the period you have already paid for. Nothing is deleted when you cancel — your progress is still there if you come back.

Refunds

Subscriptions are billed monthly in advance. We do not refund part-used months — cancel before the renewal date and you will not be charged again.

First-payment goodwill refund: if you are within 14 days of your first ever payment and have used fewer than 5 sessions on the paid plan, email us and we will refund it in full, no argument.

We will also refund a charge that was clearly a mistake — a double charge, a charge after a confirmed cancellation, or a period where the Service was substantially unavailable.

Your statutory right of withdrawal

As an EU consumer you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a distance contract. Because the Service is a digital service delivered immediately, by starting your subscription you ask for that immediate access and accept that the withdrawal right ends once delivery has begun. The goodwill refund above is offered on top of that and does not remove any right you have under Swedish or EU consumer law.

Failed payments

If a payment fails, your access continues while Stripe retries the card and you will see a notice in Settings. Update your card in Manage billing to keep the plan. If the retries run out, the account drops to the Free plan — your data stays.

How to ask

Email simmyh02@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the date of the charge. We aim to reply within 3 business days and to process approved refunds within 10 business days, back to the original payment method.
